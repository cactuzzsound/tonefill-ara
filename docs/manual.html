ToneFill
S E A M L E S S   R O O M   T O N E

ToneFill generates seamless dialogue room tone. It listens to a production clip, finds the cleanest, steadiest background ambience, and rebuilds it into a continuous bed you can stretch under any edit — no loop points, no audible joins. VST3 (with ARA) and Audio Unit for Logic Pro X.

Quick start

  1. Drop ToneFill on a dialogue clip (ARA) or region. Analysis runs automatically off the audio thread.
  2. Leave Auto on — the waveform lights the clean ambience it found in orange.
  3. Play back: the gaps and the whole region are filled with matched room tone.
  4. Not clean enough? Raise Voice Reject and lower Clean Level.
  5. Set Export Len and hit Export WAV for a standalone file.

Detection — what counts as room tone

Clean LevelAuto

How quiet a moment must be to count as room tone. Lower = stricter — rejects claps, clicks and loud bits more aggressively.

Voice RejectAuto

How hard to exclude voice, breaths and mouth noise. Higher = cleaner tone. Push it up and keep Clean Level low for dialogue-heavy takes.

Min FillAuto

Minimum length a stable fragment must have to be used. Longer = fewer, more consistent chunks and fewer audible joins.Range 0.2 – 5.0 s

Structure — how the bed is assembled

FlatnessSelection

How strict the stationarity requirement is. Higher = only very steady stretches, so the fill avoids audible jumps and level swings.

Chunk SizeBed

Length of each real chunk taken from the source. Longer = more natural texture; shorter = smoother but more repetitive.

CrossfadeBed

How much neighbouring chunks overlap and blend. Higher = smoother joins. Joins are also widened automatically when two chunks differ in the low end.

Texture — output shaping

SmoothnessEnhance

Enhance only: resynthesis window size. Higher = smoother, more diffuse; lower keeps more of the source's fine texture.

OutputLevel

Output volume in dB. Bypassed while Normalize is on.Range −24 – +24 dB

Export LenExport

Length of the WAV written by Export. The seamless loop is tiled to exactly this length.Range 0.5 – 30 s

Mode switches

Auto / Manual Auto finds clean room tone for you. Manual lets you drag your own regions on the waveform below — drag to add, click a region to remove.
Enhance / Full Enhance turns on PaulStretch resynthesis for when the real fragments won't blend into a clean bed (enables Smoothness and the Hiss Filter). Full analyses the whole take instead of the first 4 minutes — use it when clean tone is scattered across a long clip.
Classic / Experimental Classic is the tuned selection engine (default). Experimental scores every frame with a weighted model — A/B it against Classic by ear; if it doesn't win, switch back.

Hiss Filter (Enhance only)

Enhance can add a faint high-frequency hiss as it resynthesises the bed. The Hiss Filter is a live low-pass — applied in real time, so you hear the change instantly with no re-render.

Freq3–15 kHz

The corner frequency. Lower = more aggressive HF removal. Start around 9 kHz and pull down until the hiss goes.

Q0.3–2.0

The slope / resonance at the corner. Around 0.7 is a clean, gentle roll-off; higher tightens the knee.

Tip. The switch is only active while Enhance is on — with Enhance off, the real-audio bed already keeps the source's high end.

Loudness — Normalize

Turn Normalize on to bake the output to a fixed loudness target. Pick LUFS (integrated, EBU R128) or dBFS (peak), set the target with the − / + stepper, and the now readout shows the measured value. While Normalize is on, the Output knob is bypassed and the exported WAV is corrected to hit the target precisely at your chosen length.

Waveform & readouts

The bottom panel shows the whole analysed clip. Orange bars are the ambience ToneFill selected; grey is rejected. In Manual mode, drag across the waveform to add room-tone regions and click one to remove it. Expand opens a large window with a timecode ruler, zoom and scroll for precise selecting.

The status line reads:

used 25.1 s (7 chunks)  ·  avail 83.9 s  ·  seam 1.9 dB  ·  in −66 dB
usedtotal clean material actually used after the Min Fill filter, and how many chunks.
availtotal clean material found before Min Fill — if this is high but used is low, lower Min Fill.
seamaverage spectral mismatch across chunk joins (dB). A ⚠ over 4 dB means raise Min Fill or tighten the selection.
inthe analysed source level.

Actions

Regenerate rolls a new random variation of the fill with the same settings — handy if a particular realisation repeats audibly. Export WAV writes the fill to a 24-bit WAV, tiled seamlessly to Export Len (loudness-corrected when Normalize is on).

Working the low end

Room tone lives or dies below 500 Hz — that's where the ear hears a "different room". ToneFill treats the low band as a first-class part of the room's colour: chunks with a mismatched low end are rejected during selection, and joins between chunks that differ in the low end are crossfaded harder. If a fill still feels like it shifts weight at a cut, raise Min Fill and Flatness.
